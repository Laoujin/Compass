> **Decision:** A tented split (e.g. Glove80 or ZSA Voyager) with light linear
> switches — the geometry change does more for wrist strain than any switch.

## What actually moves the needle

| Factor          | Impact on fatigue | Notes                                        |
|-----------------|-------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Split + tenting | High              | Neutral wrist/forearm angle; the biggest lever |
| Switch weight   | Medium            | Lighter (35–45g) linears reduce finger load  |
| Keycap profile  | Low–Medium        | Sculpted helps; flat DSA is divisive         |
| QMK / layers    | Medium            | Fewer finger journeys once layers are muscle-memory |

The split layout is the only change testers felt within a single day; everything
else is a slow-burn preference.
