> Baratza Encore ESP if you want electric convenience at the price.

## One each

- **Quietest + most consistent:** 1Zpresso J-Ultra — hand grinder, espresso-capable, travels well.
- **Easiest daily driver:** Baratza Encore ESP — electric, good pour-over, serviceable parts.
- **Skip:** anything still using blades, or "burr-like" marketing on a flat-blade unit.
