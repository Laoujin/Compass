When to reach for which. The two-mode rule.

If the artifact has to survive the read

Use Scout.

Anything you'll cite, quote, or revisit later. Per-claim URL provenance, an Atlas you can re-read in three months, and cost that scales with your tokens — not someone else's pricing tier. The runner is the price of admission; the artifact pays you back every time you cite it.

If you'll consume the output and forget it

Use ChatGPT Deep Research.

One-off synthesis questions where you want a tight, well-paced answer and don't need to bring the receipts. The prose lands. Skim, decide, move on. Don't reach for it when you'll need to defend a claim a week later — the citations won't bear weight.
