> **Decision:** Scout for any artifact you'll cite later (full URL provenance);
> ChatGPT Deep Research for one-off synthesis you'll consume and forget.

## Quick verdict

| Tool | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|
| Scout | Per-claim citations; auditable Atlas; low marginal cost | Requires your own runner |
| ChatGPT DR | Cleanest synthesis prose; best ranking | Citations are loose; opaque cost |
| Gemini DR | Widest source coverage; multimodal | Verbose; weak structure |
| Perplexity | Fastest turnaround | Shallow on long-form |
