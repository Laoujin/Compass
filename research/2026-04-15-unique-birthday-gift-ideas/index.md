> **Pick:** A weekend at a "stargazing cabin" (B&B + Bortle-2 sky)
> beats any object she'll have to dust.

## Three angles, one each

- **Bespoke craft** — a custom piece from a Belgian maker she already follows on Instagram (think: ceramics, perfumery, leather).
- **Shared experience** — a 2-day trip to a niche destination booked end-to-end so she doesn't lift a finger.
- **Useful indulgence** — a one-time service that removes a chore she hates (deep clean, wardrobe edit, hair colorist she's been meaning to try).
