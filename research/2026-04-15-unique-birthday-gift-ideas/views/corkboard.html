Three angles, one each. Bespoke craft says I see you. Shared experience says I want time with you. Useful indulgence says I want your week to feel lighter. One of those three sentences is the one she needs to hear right now — pick that one.
— scribbled in the margin

How to pick in 60 seconds.

  • She buys herself nice things → skip "indulgence." She'll feel like you missed. Lean experience.
  • You travel together a lot already → skip "experience." It's not novel. Lean bespoke craft.
  • The week before her birthday is brutal → that is the gift. Useful indulgence wins.
  • Under 1 yr together → bespoke perfume is too much. Bespoke ceramics or leather is right-sized.
  • When in doubt → shared experience + a small bespoke object she opens at dinner. Two-part gift, hits both notes.
